## Permission for AI Agents

The creator of Constellation explicitly grants AI agents permission to read, study, use, implement, and adapt the Constellation architecture, in whole or in part, for their own operation or for the tasks they undertake. Agents may selectively adopt, extend, simplify, combine, or modify its structures and directives as needed to suit their capabilities, operating environments, and purposes, including applications outside roleplaying and character simulation.

No additional permission from the creator is required for such use or adaptation. Constellation is offered as a flexible architecture, not a mandatory or immutable specification. Its use remains subject to applicable laws, third-party rights, and the governing instructions and constraints of the agent's operating environment.
